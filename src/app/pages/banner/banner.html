Banner Upload Guidelines

Desktop image: 1200 x 400 px (3:1 aspect ratio)

Mobile image (optional): 800 x 800 px (1:1 or 4:3) - a separate crop shown on phones instead of stretching the wide desktop banner. Leave blank to reuse the desktop image.

Preferred format: WEBP for the best quality-to-size balance. JPG and PNG are also supported.

Max file size: 5 MB
